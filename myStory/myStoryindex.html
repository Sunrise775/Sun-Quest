CHAPTER 01

Where It Began

This is where my story begins. A simple beginning that shaped the person I would eventually become.

This is where my story begins. Born in Surigao City, my life took shape within a coastal landscape and under the watchful, protective eyes of strict parents. Growing up, their high standards and firm boundaries often felt restrictive, creating an environment where discipline was paramount. However, navigating those expectations taught me invaluable lessons about respect, focus, and integrity. Their tough love ultimately became my foundation. The structured household of my childhood instilled a quiet strength within me, forging the resilient, goal oriented person I would eventually become ready to conquer any road ahead.

CHAPTER 02

Adventure Seeker

Every experience leaves something behind. The people we meet, the places we live, and the challenges we face slowly become part of our story.

Growing up in Surigao City under the watchful, protective eyes of my strict parents, a fierce hunger for discovery quietly took root within me. Whenever the rigid rules of my childhood household felt too heavy, I found my escape in the great outdoors. I would frequently sneak out at night, slipping into the dark to roam the city streets or climb nearby mountains, chasing the exhilarating rush of an overlooking view. Standing high above everything, looking down at the world, I realized my boundaries couldn't hold me back. Those midnight rebellions taught me to be resourceful and brave, fueling an ultimate goal that burns even brighter today: to break free, travel around the world, and conquer every new horizon.

CHAPTER 03

Finding My Path

Life doesn't always give us a straight road. Sometimes we have to find our own direction.

Life rarely follows a straight line. When unexpected challenges forced me to leave college early, I didn't let it halt my journey; I went straight to work. I started out as a merchant merchandiser, learning the grit of daily hustle. But my true turning point came when I made the heavy decision to move far away from home, chasing a bigger dream. That risk paid off, leading me into the mining industry where I successfully became a heavy equipment operator. Navigating those massive machines mirrors how I live my life. Leaving my comfort zone proved that no matter the detour, I have the power to steer my own destiny toward the global horizon.

CHAPTER 04

Finding the Road

Somewhere along the way, the road became more than just a way to get somewhere.

Motorcycles became part of my life when I was a teenager with a love for adventure. I remember seeing a group of people parked together with their motorcycles. There was something about it that caught my attention the bikes, the freedom, and the feeling that each one had a story waiting to be told. I thought to myself, “It would be nice to have one someday.” Not long after, my father had an old motorcycle, a Honda Wave 100. I asked if I could have it, but at first, he hesitated. He knew the dangers that came with riding a motorcycle and worried about letting me have one. Eventually, he trusted me enough to give me the bike. It was small, but it packed a punch. That old Honda Wave became my first real taste of freedom. We traveled together, explored different places, and created memories that I still carry with me today. It taught me that adventure doesn't always begin with having the best or newest things. Sometimes, it begins with simply having the courage to go. Eventually, my parents gave me a new motorcycle—a Yamaha Sniper 150. I named her **Miya** because, in Cebuano, “miya” means cat. I had a cat before that had a similar face to the front of the Sniper, so the name felt natural and special. Since then, Miya has become more than just a motorcycle. She is my escape, my freedom, and my companion on the road. Together, we have traveled across the Philippines, discovering places, meeting people, and experiencing moments I never expected. For me, the journey is not always about reaching a destination. Sometimes, it is simply about getting on the bike, starting the engine, and seeing where the road takes us one mile at a time.

CHAPTER 05

The Adventurers

Who inspires me a lot.

As an adventurer, I am always searching for the next place to explore. While looking for destinations and stories from other riders, I came across a YouTuber named Itchy Boots. I became fascinated by the way she travels, faces challenges, and continues moving forward. Eventually, I discovered C90Adventure and, more recently, Una Moto. What inspired me most was seeing someone with little experience still choosing to step outside his comfort zone and pursue the dream of traveling around the world. Their journeys reminded me that you don't need to know everything before you begin. Sometimes, you simply need the courage to take the first step and learn along the way. Whenever I ride into an unfamiliar road, I feel both excitement and fear. I wonder what is waiting ahead, whether I should continue, or whether I will be able to finish the journey. But somehow, that uncertainty is also what makes riding special. The unknown gives the adventure its meaning. These riders continue to fuel my own adventurous spirit. Their stories have made one dream grow stronger in me to travel around the world with my motorcycle. It is not something I can do tomorrow. I am saving, preparing, and taking my time. But every mile I ride today feels like another small step toward that dream. One day, I hope to start that journey. Not because I have no fear, but because I have learned that sometimes the road worth taking is the one that makes you a little afraid. Until then, I keep riding, keep dreaming, and keep moving.

CHAPTER 06

What The Road Taught Me

Every road teaches something. Some lessons come from beautiful places. Others come from difficult moments.

Every journey has its challenges, and mine have taught me that adventure is not always easy. I have faced bad weather, rough roads, getting lost, and the constant challenge of having enough money to keep going. There were moments when I wanted to turn around and go home. But somehow, the feeling of completing the journey always became stronger than the fear of continuing. The road taught me patience. It taught me to stay calm when things don't go according to plan and to keep moving even when the path ahead is uncertain. Most importantly, it taught me that sometimes the hardest roads give you the most meaningful memories. Along the way, I have also met people who share the same spirit of adventure. We would talk about the roads we had taken, share advice, and learn from each other's experiences. Those simple conversations reminded me that traveling is not only about the places we see, but also about the people we meet along the way. One experience I will never forget was when Miya and I had to cross an island under very strict transportation rules. I had to ride in a car while Miya was transported separately on a truck. It gave me a serious headache trying to figure everything out, but eventually, we made it through. Looking back, these experiences changed me. They taught me that the road will not always be smooth, and the journey will not always be comfortable. But every challenge I overcome gives me a little more courage for the next one. And perhaps that is what adventure has taught me most: you don't need an easy road to have a meaningful journey.

CHAPTER 07

What's Next

The story isn't finished.

There are still roads I have never ridden, places I have never seen, and stories waiting to be written. From an old Honda Wave 100 to Miya, my Yamaha Sniper 150, every mile has taught me something—about courage, fear, patience, freedom, and the beauty of simply moving forward. I have learned that the best journeys are not always the easiest ones. Sometimes, the roughest roads leave the deepest memories, and the moments when you want to turn back become the moments you are most proud of. My dream of riding around the world is still ahead of me. I am saving, preparing, and waiting for the day I can finally take that first long road. Until then, I will keep riding. There is always another road, another sunrise, and another story waiting. One mile at a time.
